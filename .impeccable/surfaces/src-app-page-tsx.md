# Surface brief: UTM Esports landing page

Scope: `/` (src/app/page.tsx). Mode: Persuade. Redesign: the Lane Lines and the dark split hero are replaced; content, features and constraints stay.

Audience and job: prospective players (students, on phones) apply for tryouts; fans join the Discord; sponsors see a credible org and send a partner enquiry. Primary action: Join the squad (Google Form). Secondary: Join our Discord. Proof: real photos, real roster, the Top 4 at NinjaPrint's 6th Anniversary MLBB Tournament (March 2025, first face-to-face tournament); no other results.

Requirements kept (from the team's plan and the user): static export, no database, forms via Google Forms, first load under 1 MB, WCAG 2.2 AA with reduced motion, mobile first with sticky Discord button on phones, content in one typed file, content keyed by game (MLBB, later Valorant, League of Legends, Honor of Kings), roster without roles or captain, hero picture shuffle (squad photo, Crimson Cloudburst, Sakura Blossom), next event with countdown, About with mission, vision, story and results, sponsors strip (hidden until sponsors exist) and Partner with us, textures, low-opacity dragon background (UTM LOGO 2), red, black and white, not corporate or cold.

Memorable moment: the red field, the sheared type and the cut section edges; the hero picture shuffle shows the squad and both full jersey graphics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The logo's own cuts become the page: a red field, sheared italic type, parallelogram buttons and plates, and shallow slashed section edges. The user rejected slicing the hero photo, so the squad and jerseys sit in a plain whole-picture frame. It refuses pills, badges and card rows.

OWN-WORLD: Ground is the logo's red gradient, deep crimson (#6b0f12) at top left to bright red (#e4181e) at bottom right; white for type and the knife lines; warm ink (#16090a) for text on white slabs and for the footer. A dark-red ghost layer (#5e0c10, faint) sits behind, like the logo's shadow dragon. Display type is a wide, heavy, forward-leaning italic in capitals, like the UTM wordmark; body is plain Barlow. Every block, photo frame and button is a parallelogram cut at the jaw angle (about 14 degrees). No cards, no rounded corners, no pills; content is divided by slashes and gaps.

STORY: A visitor on a phone sees a confident team that welcomes new players, sees the real squad and the Top 4 through the cuts, understands how to try out, and taps Join the squad or Join our Discord.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A solid ink nav bar (logo, links, Discord, Join the squad). Below it, on the red field, the sheared white headline, a short line and the Join the squad button on the left, and the picture shuffle in a plain 16:9 frame on the right with its caption written on the picture. Phones: headline and button first, then the picture.
